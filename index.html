How to Install on Android

  1. 01
    Tap Download APK

    The APK file will download to your device's Downloads folder.

  2. 02
    Open the file

    Go to Files / My Files and tap bear-microg-*.apk to begin installation.

  3. 03
    Allow unknown sources

    Android will prompt you — tap Settings and enable "Install unknown apps" for your file manager or browser, then go back and tap Install.

  4. 04
    Launch Bear MicroG

    Tap Open after installation completes. Sign in with your Google account to get started.
